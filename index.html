About Eden

Exceptional care, delivered with kindness, patience and respect.

Eden Home Health Services LLC is a Texas-based home care agency dedicated to compassionate, reliable and personalized non-medical care for people who need assistance with everyday living.

We help seniors, individuals with disabilities and those who need additional support keep their independence, dignity and comfort in the familiar surroundings of their own homes.

Inviting someone into your home to care for you or a loved one is a significant decision. We earn that trust through professionalism, individual attention and real relationships with every client and family.
